Ready in under a minute.

Install it once, grant macOS the permissions it needs, and make sure it launches automatically whenever you sign in.

1

Unzip

Extract the downloaded MouseLocator.zip file and drag MouseLocator.app into your Applications folder.

2

Bypass Gatekeeper

Double-click the app. If macOS says "Apple could not verify…", click Done. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

3

Grant Permissions

In System Settings → Privacy & Security, add MouseLocator to both:

• Accessibility — needed to move the mouse.
• Input Monitoring — needed for the hotkey.

4

Launch Automatically

Open System Settings → General → Login Items. Under Open at Login, click the + button, choose MouseLocator.app from your Applications folder, and confirm.
