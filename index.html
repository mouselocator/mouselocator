Cursor control center

Ready in under a minute.

Install it once, grant macOS the permissions it needs, and make sure it launches automatically whenever you sign in.

1

Unzip

Extract the downloaded MouseLocator.zip file and drag MouseLocator.app into your Applications folder.

2

Bypass Gatekeeper

Double-click the app. If macOS says "Apple could not verify…", click Done. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

3

Grant Permissions

In System Settings → Privacy & Security, add MouseLocator to both:

• Accessibility — needed to move the mouse.
• Input Monitoring — needed for the hotkey.

4

Launch Automatically

Open System Settings → General → Login Items. Under Open at Login, click the + button, choose MouseLocator.app from your Applications folder, and confirm.

Cleanup

How to uninstall.

Need to remove MouseLocator? Follow these simple steps to ensure it is completely deleted from your system.

1

Remove Permissions

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and remove permissions for MouseLocator.

2

Open Activity Monitor

Press Cmd + Space, type Activity Monitor, and hit Enter.

3

Search

In the top-right search bar of Activity Monitor, type MouseLocator.

4

Highlight

Click on the MouseLocator process in the list to highlight it.

5

Stop the Process

Click the "X" button at the very top of the Activity Monitor window (an octagon with an X).

6

Force Quit

Click Force Quit on the confirmation popup to terminate the background process.

7

Move to Trash

Open Finder, navigate to your Applications folder, and move MouseLocator to the Trash.
