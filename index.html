Cursor control center

Ready in under a minute.

Install it once, grant macOS the permissions it needs, and let your cursor stop hiding.

⇩
1

Unzip

Extract the downloaded MouseLocator.zip file and drag MouseLocator.app into your Applications folder.

⌘
2

Bypass Gatekeeper

Double-click the app. If macOS says “Apple could not verify…”, click Done. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the security section, and click Open Anyway.

✓
3

Grant Permissions

In System Settings → Privacy & Security, add MouseLocator to both:

• Accessibility — needed to move the mouse.
• Input Monitoring — needed for the hotkey.
