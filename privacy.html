Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MouseLocator ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and software application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

Website Analytics

Our website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service that:

  • Does NOT use cookies or persistent identifiers
  • Does NOT collect personal data or track users across sites
  • Aggregates all data anonymously
  • Is fully GDPR and CCPA compliant without requiring consent banners

Cloudflare may collect:

  • Page views and referring URLs
  • Device type and browser information (aggregated)
  • Approximate location (country/region level only)

App Telemetry (Optional)

The MouseLocator application may collect anonymous usage data to improve stability and performance:

  • App launch events (to measure active users)
  • Crash reports (to identify and fix bugs)
  • Feature usage statistics (aggregated)

This data is:

  • Anonymous — no personal identifiers, email addresses, or device IDs
  • Aggregated — we only see totals and trends, not individual users
  • Optional — you can disable telemetry in the app settings if provided

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Understand how users interact with our website and app
  • Identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • Improve the user experience and add requested features
  • Monitor app performance and stability

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data. We may share aggregated, anonymous data with:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — for website traffic analysis
  • PostHog or similar services — for app telemetry (if enabled)

These third parties are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

4. Data Retention

We retain analytics and telemetry data for as long as necessary to provide and improve our services. Aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for statistical purposes.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain data processing
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent)

Since we collect minimal anonymous data, these rights may be limited. Contact us if you have questions.

6. Children's Privacy

MouseLocator is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the MouseLocator website or repository.

9. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using information is:

  • Legitimate interests — to improve our software and provide better user experience
  • Consent — where required by law (e.g., optional telemetry features)
